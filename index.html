✦Simple Rules✦

Only successfully completed trades with Puff count. Cancelled, rejected, failed, refunded or disputed trades do not count.

The challenge runs from Nov 20, 12:00 AM to Nov 26, 11:59 PM. A trade counts for the time it is completed.

Rank is based on the total value of your completed trades.

If two traders have the same total, the one who reached it first ranks higher.

Daily winners get Points the next day. Top 30 winners get Points after the challenge ends.

Prizes not won (for example, fewer than 30 people reach 3,500 Points) are not carried over.

The leaderboard on this page is updated once a day. Final results come from Puff's trade records.

Puff may disqualify anyone who breaks these rules. Puff's decision is final.

🛡️

Stay safe: Puff will never ask for your password, PIN, or any payment to claim a prize. Anyone who does is a scammer.
